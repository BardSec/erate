About USAC Import

This tool pulls public E-rate data from the USAC Open Data platform. It can import:

  • Funding Years — NSLP %, discount rates, enrollment
  • FRNs — Funding request numbers, amounts, status
  • Vendors — Service providers with SPIN numbers
  • C2 Budgets — Category 2 budget allocations
Tip: You can find your Entity Number (BEN) on any previous E-rate filing or at usac.org.
